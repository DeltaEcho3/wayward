Big Buck Bunny

10:35AnimationOpen movie

A gentle giant of a rabbit, three troublemaking rodents and one very bad day in the forest.

This week's pick is a classic from the open-movie world. Big Buck Bunny was made by the Blender Foundation with free software, and anyone may share it. It's short, funny and perfect for a night in the tent with a phone propped against a water bottle.

Watching offline

No signal at camp? Save it before you leave home, pick a smaller size if your phone is nearly full, and you're set for the trip.
