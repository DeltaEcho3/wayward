## What Wayward is

Wayward is a private browser for Android. It blocks ads and trackers, keeps your downloads behind your phone's lock, and has an AI assistant that runs on your own xAI, Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter key. Wayward is closed source. This repository holds its website, help, release notes and issue tracker, plus the source of the open-source parts it ships.

## Get it

[Wayward Browser on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.deltaechothree.wayward). It is available in the United States only.

## What it does

- **Blocks ads and trackers** on every page, in regular and private tabs, at the level you pick: Standard, Strict or Maximum.
- **Saves videos to watch later.** Pick the quality before you save.
- **Keeps downloads private.** They stay inside Wayward, out of your gallery, behind your fingerprint, face or PIN, unless you choose a folder of your own.
- **Has an AI assistant that uses your own key** (Premium). Ask about a page, search the web or compare your open tabs. You decide how much it asks you first, and a receipt shows what went to your provider.
- **Keeps things apart.** Private tabs save no history, spaces can keep their own sign-ins, and a tab, a space or your private tabs can be locked.
- **Cleans up after itself.** Exit Wayward asks first, then deletes your tabs, history and sign-ins and closes the app. Bookmarks, downloads and settings stay. Dark mode darkens websites too.

## Privacy in short

Wayward doesn't send us your browsing history, the sites you visit, your downloads, your cookies or page contents. There are no ads, analytics or trackers in the app. When you use the assistant, what you share goes to the AI provider you picked, with your own key, and not to us. The full details are in the [privacy policy](https://deltaecho3.github.io/wayward/privacy.html).

## Premium

Premium is a one-time purchase through Google Play, not a subscription. It unlocks the AI assistant, alternate app icons, extra color themes and a supporter badge. Your AI provider bills you separately for what the assistant uses. Ad and tracker blocking, the privacy shields, video downloads and the downloads lock are free.

## Help and feedback

The [help](https://deltaecho3.github.io/wayward/help/) has how-tos, walkthroughs and answers. To tell us something from the app, open **Settings** > **Send feedback**: you see exactly what will be sent, and you send it from your own email app. You can also write to trywayward@gmail.com.

## Report a bug

[Open an issue](https://github.com/DeltaEcho3/wayward/issues/new/choose) and pick the form that fits: a bug, a site that breaks, or an idea. Issues are public, so leave out anything private, such as addresses of pages you wouldn't want others to see, account names or keys.

## Security

To report a security problem, please don't open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Open-source parts

Wayward includes open-source software. The sources their licenses ask us to offer, exactly as Wayward uses them, are in [oss/](oss/) ([as a page](https://deltaecho3.github.io/wayward/oss/)). The full list of components and licenses is in the app under **Settings** > **About Wayward** > **Legal information** > **Open-source licenses**.
