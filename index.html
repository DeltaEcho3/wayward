Ads and trackers blocked.

On every page, at the level you pick: Standard, Strict or Maximum.

Delete, then exit.

Exit Wayward asks first, then deletes your tabs, history and sign-ins and closes. Bookmarks, downloads and settings stay.

Wayward doesn't send us your browsing history, the sites you visit, your downloads, your cookies or page contents. There are no ads, analytics or trackers in the app.
