Wayward

Browse your own way. A private Android browser built on Mozilla's open-source GeckoView engine, with real ad and tracker blocking, private video downloads, and an optional AI assistant that asks before it reads a page.

Wayward doesn't send us your browsing history, the sites you visit, your downloads, your cookies or page contents. There are no ads, analytics or trackers in the app.
