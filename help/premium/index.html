Premium questions

What does Premium include?

Premium includes the AI assistant, extra home-screen icons (Wayward Dark, Wayward Mono, Wayward Mono Dark and Browser), extra colors (Midnight, Ocean, Sunset, Neon and Mono), and a supporter badge on your About screen. Everything else in Wayward is free and stays free: ad and tracker blocking, Shields, private tabs, spaces, locks, video downloads and the downloads lock. See Unlock Premium, or get it back.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once through Google Play, and it's yours from then on. The Premium page shows the price, as Google Play gives it in your currency.

Does Premium pay for the AI assistant's answers?

No. Premium unlocks the assistant. You use your own account with an AI provider (OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI or xAI), and that provider bills you for what you use, at its own prices. You can set a spending limit with your provider. See Keys and costs.

Can I use Premium on my other phones?

Yes. Sign in to Google Play on each Android device with the account you used for the purchase. Install Wayward there, and Premium unlocks once Google Play confirms your purchase (or tap Restore purchase on the Premium page).

Does Premium work without a connection?

Yes. Wayward checks with Google Play when it can connect. Premium stays active for 30 days after the last time Play confirmed it, so a Play outage or a phone with no signal doesn't turn it off. After 30 days without hearing from Play, Premium pauses until Play confirms your purchase again. Its features are off meanwhile, but nothing is deleted or reset: the assistant's settings and chat, your icon and your color all stay as they are (a Premium color shows as Wayward's own until then).

Can I get a refund?

Request a refund through Google Play. Its refund policies apply. See section 5 of the terms.

What happens if Premium ends, after a refund?

Premium features turn off. Wayward disables the assistant and deletes its chat, and your icon and color go back to Wayward's own. That only happens when Google Play reports a refund or a cancellation; 30 days without hearing from Play only pauses Premium (see Does Premium work without a connection?). Your saved tasks can still be renamed and deleted in Settings > AI assistant, and a chat you saved to a file can still be read there with Open saved chat.

Why do some colors and icons have a lock?

A lock marks a Premium color or icon. Tapping one opens the Premium page, so you can see what's included first.

What's the supporter badge?

It's a thank-you badge on your About Wayward screen, under Wayward's name, and on the Premium page. It says Founding supporter for buyers in Wayward's early days, and Supporter after that.
