Looks, dark mode and text

Where is dark mode?

Open the ⋮ menu and tap Dark mode, the last row of the second card. It makes Wayward and websites dark together, and tapping it again makes both light. You can also add a round Dark button to the menu's top row. See Turn on dark mode.

Why is a website still white when Wayward is dark?

Picking dark makes websites dark too, whether you tap the menu's Dark mode or pick Dark under Theme in Settings > Themes and app icon. If a site is still white:

  • A tab that was already open changes the next time it loads.
  • The site may be kept light: tap the blue shield and turn on Dark on this site.
  • Website appearance may be Light: it's in Settings > Accessibility.

See Turn on dark mode.

A darkened site looks wrong. How do I keep just that site light?

Open the site, tap the blue shield in the address bar, then turn off Dark on this site. The page loads again in its own light look, and stays light while other sites are dark. See Keep one site light.

I turned dark mode off and my color went back to blue. Why?

Midnight is always dark, so turning Dark mode off also moves the color back to Wayward's own blue. Otherwise the switch could never be off. Pick another color in Settings > Themes and app icon.

How do I make text on pages bigger?

Go to Settings > Accessibility. Turn off Follow your phone's text size and drag Text size, from 50% to 200%. Wayward's own menus follow your phone's font size instead. See Make text bigger, or zoom any site.

A site won't let me zoom. Can I zoom anyway?

Yes. Turn on Zoom on every site in Settings > Accessibility, so you can pinch to zoom on every page.

Can I have the address bar at the top?

Yes. Go to Settings > Customize > At the top. The buttons stay at the bottom, nearest your thumb. With the Minimalist Address Bar, the whole bar goes to the top. See Change the bottom bar's buttons.

Where did the AI button go?

If you assigned a different action to the middle button, or said No thanks to the assistant in the setup, the assistant is off the bar and hidden everywhere else too. Bring it back with Settings > Customize > Middle button > AI assistant. Settings > AI assistant also says The assistant is hidden, and tapping that brings it back. Your chat is kept.

Can I put everything back the way Wayward came?

Settings > Customize > Reset to default restores the original bars, menu and gestures as they came, immediately. It doesn't touch your theme, your home page or your other settings. To walk through the main choices again, see Run the setup again.

I changed the app icon, but my home screen still shows the old one.

Your home screen may take a moment to update the icon. Wait a few seconds, or go to another home screen page and back. If you'd pinned shortcuts from Wayward to your home screen, you may need to add those again.

Why do some colors and icons have a lock?

Those colors and icons require Premium. Tapping one shows what Premium includes. See What does Premium include?
