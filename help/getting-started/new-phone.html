Move Wayward to a new phone

How-to

Before switching phones, save the bookmarks, settings and downloads you want to keep. Premium comes with you to the new phone.

Wayward keeps everything on your phone, and none of it goes with your phone's backup or a phone-to-phone transfer. That's on purpose: your history, tabs and downloads never leave your phone unless you move them yourself. So do these steps on the old phone first.

On your old phone

  1. Back up your settings: Settings > Backup > Settings > Save to a file. Save it somewhere you can reach from the new phone, like your cloud drive or a computer.
  2. Export your bookmarks: Settings > Backup > Bookmarks > Export (or Bookmarks > three dots > Export bookmarks), and save that file the same way.
  3. If you use the assistant, Settings > Backup > Chats > Save chat keeps your chat as an encrypted file. On the new phone, Open a chat reads it.
  4. Move the downloads you want to keep. Downloads kept inside Wayward stay inside Wayward, so open Settings > Downloads and tap Move downloads to a folder, or move single ones from their menu in Downloads with Move to folder. Then copy that folder to the new phone the way you copy photos.
  5. Keep the tabs you want: open tabs don't move, so bookmark the ones you'll want again.

More about each file in Bring your bookmarks and settings.

On your new phone

  1. Install Wayward from Google Play, signed in to the same Google account you bought Premium with (if you did).
  2. Open Wayward. On the setup's Bring your things page, use Restore a backup with your settings file, and Import bookmarks with your bookmarks file.
  3. Premium comes back by itself once Google Play confirms your purchase. If it doesn't, open Settings > Premium and tap Restore purchase. See Unlock Premium, or get it back.
  4. If you use the AI assistant, add your key again in Settings > AI assistant. A key is never in a backup.
  5. Set up anything else that isn't in the backup: locks, Wayward's language, the home-screen icon, your wallpaper, and signing back in to sites.

What can't move

  • Your history, open tabs and site sign-ins.
  • Saved tasks and the assistant's chat. To keep a chat, save it to a file from the assistant first: see Save, open or delete a chat.
  • Start page shortcuts you added, and the shortcuts on the private start page: neither is in a backup. Add them again, or keep them as bookmarks.
