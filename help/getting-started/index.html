Installing and updating

Which phones does Wayward work on?

Wayward runs on phones and tablets running Android 10 or later. Wayward comes from Google Play, and it's the same app on a phone, a tablet or a foldable: on a wide screen its bars and settings keep a comfortable width.

How does Wayward update?

Google Play handles Wayward updates, like your other apps. To check manually, open Settings > Rate Wayward: it opens Wayward's page in the Play Store, which shows an Update button when there's a new version. Wayward's built-in parts, like its video downloader, update along with the app. The ad blocker's filter lists update on their own in between.

Will an update keep my tabs, bookmarks and downloads?

Yes. Updating keeps everything. Uninstalling is different: Android then deletes everything Wayward keeps on your phone, including downloads kept inside Wayward. Before you uninstall, move the downloads you want to a folder, and save your bookmarks and settings to files. See Move Wayward to a new phone.

Which version do I have?

Open Settings. The last row, About Wayward, shows your version. Tap it, then tap Application version to copy the version along with your Android version, handy when you write to us. You'll see Version copied.

Do I need an account?

No. Wayward has no account and no sign-in. Premium is bought with your Google Play account, and the AI assistant uses your own account with an AI provider, but browsing needs neither.

Is Wayward free?

Yes. You can browse for free. Ad and tracker blocking, Shields, private tabs, spaces, video downloads and the downloads lock are free. Premium is an optional one-time purchase for the AI assistant, extra app icons and color themes: see What's included in Premium.
