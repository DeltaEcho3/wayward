Problems and questions

Wayward crashed. What should I do?

Reopen Wayward. To report the crash, go to Settings > Send feedback, and pick The app crashed. That report includes where in Wayward's code the crash happened (never your pages or addresses), which is what we need most. Wayward keeps only the last crash on your phone, so send it before another one replaces it. See Send feedback or report a problem.

Can I install add-ons or extensions?

No. Wayward doesn't support add-ons or extensions. An add-on can read and change every page you visit, so leaving them out keeps Wayward safer and simpler to keep up to date. The things people usually install them for are built in instead:

  • Ad and tracker blocking, with uBlock Origin built in: Settings > Shields & Privacy.
  • Hiding pop-ups and other annoyances: see the next question.
  • Hiding one part of a page you don't want to see: tap the blue shield, then Ad blocking controls, and use its element picker.
  • Dark mode for every site: see Turn on dark mode.
  • Translating pages on your phone, and reader view: in the menu's top row.
  • Filling in passwords: your phone's password manager does it. Settings > Passwords and passkeys (Password manager on Android 13 and older) opens your phone's page for it.

How do I stop pop-ups that cover the page?

To enable the pop-up filter list, go to Settings > Shields & Privacy > Filter lists, then under Annoyances turn on AdGuard – Popup Overlays. Or set Ad blocking to Maximum, which turns on that list and the other annoyance lists (chat boxes, newsletter and notification prompts) together. More lists can break a site now and then: if one does, turn off Protection on this site for it. See Hide pop-ups and overlays on pages and Fix a site that looks broken or won't work.

Can I add a filter list by its web address?

Not on Wayward's own Filter lists screen, which turns uBlock Origin's own lists on and off, including regional lists for many languages. uBlock Origin's own dashboard can import a list from its address: tap the blue shield, then Ad blocking controls, and open its dashboard from there. Wayward keeps lists you add there when you change levels. Add only lists you trust, since a list can break sites. See Can I add a filter list by its address, or write my own rules?

Why does Wayward ask for my fingerprint, face or PIN?

Your private tabs and your downloads are locked by default, so someone holding your phone can't open them. A new private tab asks too, even when it's your first. Turn the locks off, or set how quickly they lock again, in Settings > Tabs and History (Lock private tabs, Locked tabs lock again) and Settings > Downloads (Lock downloads). Turning a lock off asks for your unlock first. See Lock a tab, your private tabs, a space or Downloads.

Is there an Exit button?

Yes: Exit Wayward, the last row of the ⋮ menu's lower card (it's in the Tabs screen's ⋮ menu too, and under More… with the Minimalist Address Bar). It asks first. Delete everything and exit deletes your tabs, history, sign-ins, site permissions, the assistant's chat and the start page shortcuts you added, then closes Wayward and takes it out of recent apps; bookmarks, downloads and settings stay. A download under way keeps going. See clear data and delete everything.

To close Wayward and keep your tabs, swipe it away in your phone's recent apps instead. Private tabs forget their cookies and site data when the last one closes, or when Wayward closes. To have everything deleted each time you swipe it away, turn on Delete everything when Wayward closes in Settings > Tabs and History.

Does Wayward collect anything about me?

Wayward contains no ads, analytics or trackers, and it doesn't send us your history, the sites you visit, your downloads, your cookies or what's on your pages. Feedback is only sent when you send it yourself, and you see all of it first. The AI assistant is the exception you choose: what you share with it goes to the AI provider you picked. The details, including every service Wayward connects to, are in the privacy policy.

Wayward is using a lot of my phone's storage. What can I clear?

  • Downloaded files usually take up the most space. The top of Downloads shows how much they use. Delete what you don't need, or have old ones deleted for you with Settings > Downloads > Delete saved files after.
  • Translation files: Settings > Languages > Delete translation files. They download again when you next translate.
  • Saved parts of pages: Clear browsing data > Cached images and files. Pages may load a little slower until they're saved again.

Does Wayward use a lot of my phone's memory?

Wayward saves memory by itself. When you go to another app, it closes the pages of your background tabs, apart from pinned tabs, the tab you're on and a few others, and they load again when you open them. Its video downloader starts only when you need it, and uBlock Origin waits until you've used Wayward for a few minutes before it updates its lists. The blocking level is one memory cost you choose: Strict and Maximum add the big URLhaus malware list, which Standard leaves out. Closing tabs you're done with helps too. See how Wayward saves memory.

Why did my tab reload after I came back to Wayward?

To free memory, Wayward closes background tabs' pages when you leave, and Android may end Wayward itself. Your tabs and your place on each page come back, and text you hadn't sent usually does too. Pin a tab to keep its page loaded. See pages that stay loaded.
