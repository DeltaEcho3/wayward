When a download fails

How-to

If your download stops or never starts, here's how to get it going, and what its message means.

First, retry

Open Downloads (the arrow button on the bar). A failed download says why on its row. Tap its three dots and then Retry. To retry several, hold one to start selecting, tap the others, then tap Retry in the bar at the bottom.

If it fails again, find its message below.

What the message means

  • The link expired or the site refused it. Open the page again and try once more. Video addresses often only work for a short time. Tap the download's three dots, then Source to open its page again, and download it from there.
  • Parts of the video didn't download. Tap Retry. Some pieces didn't arrive, so Wayward didn't keep a broken file. Retry fetches it again.
  • This space keeps its sign-ins apart, so they couldn't be used. Try it from Main. The video needs your sign-in, and a space with its own sign-ins can't send it with the download. Open the page in Main and download it there.
  • This isn't a video file. The site may have sent a web page, or the link may have expired. Open the page again and download from it.
  • Open Wayward, then tap Retry: the download needs Wayward open. Open it and tap Retry.
  • The PDF is gone; save the page as a PDF again: open the page and use Save as PDF in the menu again.
  • Not saved: it's an app file. Tap its link to download it. Wayward only keeps an app file (an .apk) when you asked for it. Tap the link on the page, and Wayward asks Download this app? first.
  • Continues when you open Wayward: Android closed Wayward while the download ran, and this download needs Wayward open to go on. It keeps what it has and carries on as soon as you open Wayward.
  • Waiting for connection: you're offline. It goes on by itself when you're back online.
  • Waiting for one to finish: you've reached your limit of downloads at once. Change it in Settings > Downloads > Downloads at once.
  • This site can't resume, so the download started again. After a pause, the site sent the whole file again instead of the rest.

When the video panel has no download button

The panel that opens from the tab above the address bar says why:

  • Play the video first, then download it. Some players only load their video once it plays. Play it for a moment, then open the panel again.
  • This video is protected and can't be downloaded. The site protects it with copy protection (DRM). Wayward never gets around copy protection, so it can't be saved. You can still watch it.
  • Wayward can't save this player's video. The page builds the video in a way Wayward can't save. Playing it still works.

Some sites can't be downloaded from at all in the Google Play version of Wayward: their videos get no download tab, and a download from one says it isn't available in this version of Wayward.

Only download what you have the right to, and follow each site's terms (see section 7 of the terms).

Still failing?

Send us a report: Settings > Send feedback, with Download problem. It includes a short code for what went wrong, never the address or the file's name. See Send feedback or report a problem. For more on downloads, see When a download doesn't work and Save a video from a page.
