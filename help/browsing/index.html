Your browser and links

How do I make Wayward my phone's browser?

Open the ⋮ menu and tap Settings. If Wayward isn't your default browser, a Make Wayward your browser card sits at the top. Tap it and answer Android's question, or pick Wayward on the Default apps page it opens. If you closed the card, use the Default browser row under Privacy instead. After that, links from other apps open in Wayward, with its blocking. See links from other apps.

Why do some links open another app, and how do I keep them in Wayward?

Some links can open an app on your phone, like a map or a shopping app. Wayward asks first: "Open in" and the app's name, naming the site the link is on. If you'd rather it never leaves Wayward, go to Settings > Shields & Privacy > Links that open apps and pick Never. The link then stays in Wayward and opens its web page if it has one. You can still hand any link to an app yourself: hold it and tap Open in app. See the privacy settings.

I shared a link to Wayward. Where did it go?

Wayward opens and shows the shared page in a new tab. Press Back on that first page and the tab closes, taking you back to the app you shared from. Otherwise the tab stays with your other tabs. Text you share without a link opens Shared text instead, where you can search for it. See links from other apps.

Why did Back take me out of Wayward?

If you opened a tab by tapping or sharing a link in another app, Back on that tab's first page closes the tab and takes you back to that app, as most browsers do. The same goes for a tab a page opened: Back on its first page closes it and returns you to the tab it came from. See links from other apps.

What's the small Wayward window that opens over other apps?

You're seeing a link preview. Some apps ask for one instead of a full browser. Tap the X (TalkBack: Close preview) or press Back to return to the app, or tap ⋮ > Open in Wayward to carry on in a full tab. See link previews.

Why does typing my router's address search instead of opening it?

An address that contains a dot and ends in letters (like example.com) opens, and so do number addresses like 192.168.1.1 and localhost. A single word, like your router's name, is searched, since it could be either. Type http:// in front of it (for example http://router) and it opens. See search or go to an address.

Can links from other apps open in a space of mine?

Yes. By default, links from other apps open in Main, with its sign-ins. Turn on Settings > Spaces > Other apps' links open in the space you're in to have them open in whichever space you're in. See spaces.

Tabs and bars

How do I get back a tab I closed by mistake?

Tap Undo when you see the message Tab closed (or Private tab closed). It stays about three seconds, longer if your phone's accessibility settings ask for more time. Swiping it away, closing another tab or leaving Wayward ends it sooner. The one time there's no Undo: with Private by default on, when you close every tab you have and some of them are private, since the new private tab has to start clean. See open, find and close tabs.

Are my tabs still there after I close Wayward?

Wayward keeps your regular tabs, including their pins, names and groups, unless you turned on Delete everything when Wayward closes (Settings > Tabs and History). Private tabs are never kept: closing Wayward ends them. Text you typed in a page and hadn't sent comes back too, if Android ended the page in the background. See open, find and close tabs.

Where did some of my tabs go?

They're probably inactive: tabs you haven't opened in 14 days, and the older copy of a page you have open twice, move to one card under the spaces, like 23 inactive tabs, and the tab count on the bar leaves them out. Nothing is closed. Tap the card, then tap a tab to bring it back, or Restore all. To turn this off, or pick after how many days, go to Settings > Tabs and History > Inactive tabs. Pinned tabs never become inactive. See inactive tabs.

Why does a tab load again when I go back to it?

When you go to another app, Wayward closes the pages of your background tabs to free your phone's memory, and Android may end Wayward too. The tab keeps your place on the page and what you'd typed in its forms, and its page loads again when you open it. See pages that stay loaded.

Can I keep a tab loaded?

Yes: pin it. Hold its card on the Tabs screen and tap Pin tab, or pin its group with Pin group. Pinned tabs and tabs in a pinned group stay loaded when you go to another app, and keep their pages longest when memory runs low. The tab you're on and a tab playing sound or video stay loaded too. If Android ends all of Wayward, every page loads again. See pages that stay loaded.

I switched apps and the page reloaded. Is what I typed gone?

Usually not. Text you typed in a page's text box and hadn't sent yet, like a message on a chat page, comes back by itself into the same box, ready to send. Wayward doesn't send it for you. This works on secure (https) pages, but not in private tabs, password, card or code boxes, or boxes the site asks browsers not to remember. See text you hadn't sent yet.

Is there an Exit button?

Yes: Exit Wayward, last in the ⋮ menu's lower card and in the Tabs screen's ⋮ menu (with the Minimalist Address Bar, under More…). After you tap Delete everything and exit, it closes every tab, private ones too, deletes your history, sign-ins, site permissions, the assistant's chat and the start page shortcuts you added, and closes Wayward and its web app windows. Bookmarks, downloads and settings stay, and the next start opens on a fresh start page. See clear data and delete everything.

To close Wayward and keep your tabs, swipe it away in your phone's recent apps, as with any app. That ends your private tabs. If you want everything else gone too each time, turn on Settings > Tabs and History > Delete everything when Wayward closes.

Why do the bars disappear when I scroll?

Full screen pages is on (Android 11 and later), so the bars slide away to give pages the whole screen. Scroll back up a little and the bars come back. To keep them in view, turn off Settings > Customize > Full screen pages. The bar then shrinks to a slim strip with the site's name as you scroll; tap it to bring the bar back. See search or go to an address.

Can I put the address bar at the top?

Yes. Go to Settings > Customize > Address bar > At the top. The buttons stay at the bottom, near your thumb. See search or go to an address.

Why does a new tab open my search engine instead of the start page?

New tabs open on is set to Search engine's page. To restore the start page, go to Settings > Tabs and History > New tabs open on > Wayward start page. See use the start page.

How do I close lots of tabs at once?

On the Tabs screen, open the ⋮ menu and tap Close all tabs (not private) (pinned tabs stay), or hold a tab, tap Select tabs, pick the ones to go and tap Delete. Both offer Undo. Inactive tabs stay: close them from the Inactive tabs screen. See pin, rename or pick several tabs.

Reading pages

Does Desktop site stay on?

Yes, for that tab. Once you tap Desktop in the ⋮ menu, the tab keeps asking sites for their desktop pages until you tap it again. A regular tab keeps it even after Wayward restarts. It's set per tab, not per site, so other tabs aren't changed. See desktop site, zoom, print and save as PDF.

When I translate a page, is its text sent anywhere?

No. Your phone translates the text locally. The only thing that goes online is a download of the language files, the first time you translate between two languages (tens of MB, from Mozilla, whose translator Wayward uses). See translate a page.

How do I make the text on pages bigger?

Go to Settings > Accessibility. Turn off Follow your phone's text size, then adjust Text size until the sample reads well. To pinch-zoom sites that turn zooming off, turn on Zoom on every site there too. See desktop site, zoom, print and save as PDF.

Why isn't Reader in the menu on this page?

Reader view is only available on pages that read as an article, with a title and paragraphs of text. Front pages, search results, shops and the start page don't. See read an article without the clutter.

How do I search for a word on the page?

Open the ⋮ menu and tap Find, then enter the word you want to find. See find words on a page.
