Bookmarks, folders, import and export

How-to

Bookmark a page to find it again later. Bookmarks stay on your phone, encrypted, and nothing clears them except deleting them yourself.

Bookmark a page

Tap the star on the bar (TalkBack: Bookmark), or open the ⋮ menu and tap Bookmark. Tap it again to remove the bookmark.

Open your bookmarks

Open the ⋮ menu and tap Bookmarks, or hold the star on the bar. Bookmarks and folders are in one list. Tap a bookmark to open its page, or a folder to open it.

To make a folder, tap the folder icon at the top (TalkBack: New folder) and give it a name.

Change, move or delete one

Tap a bookmark's ⋮:

  • Edit opens Edit bookmark, with its Name and Address.
  • Move to folder lists your folders, starting with Top level (outside any folder).
  • Delete deletes it.

A folder's ⋮ has Edit (to rename it), Move to folder, and Delete folder and its contents. The folder goes at once with Folder deleted and Undo, in case you didn't mean it.

Bring bookmarks from another browser, or save a copy

On the Bookmarks screen, tap ⋮ at the top:

  • Export bookmarks saves every bookmark and folder to an .html file. Other browsers can import that file.
  • Import bookmarks reads a bookmarks file that another browser exported (the usual .html kind) into a new folder named "Imported" and the date. It first shows a short guide: pick the .html file your other browser saved, or tap your browser (Chrome, Samsung Internet, Firefox, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera, DuckDuckGo or Vivaldi) to see how to get one. Choose file then opens your phone's file picker.

First export your bookmarks in the other browser (look for Export bookmarks in its settings or bookmarks menu), then import the file in Wayward. You can also import from the welcome setup's Bring your things page.
