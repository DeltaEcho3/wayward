Read an article without the clutter

How-to

Reader view removes menus, ads and sidebars. It shows the article text, title, author and estimated reading time, such as "4 min read".

  1. Open an article.
  2. Open the ⋮ menu and tap Reader. With the Minimalist Address Bar, tap More…, then Reader view.

You'll only see it on pages that read as an article, not on the start page or a site's front page.

Change how it reads

The toolbar at the top has:

  • "A−" and "A+" for smaller and bigger text,
  • a font button that switches between a serif and a sans font (it shows the name of the other one, "Sans" or "Serif"),
  • three "Aa" swatches for a light, sepia or dark look.

Wayward remembers what you pick for the next article.

Leave reader view

Press Back, or open the menu and tap Reader again (it's switched on while you're reading). With the Minimalist Address Bar, tap More…, then Exit reader view. The page comes back as it was.
