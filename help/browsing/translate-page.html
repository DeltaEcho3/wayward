Translate a page

How-to

Wayward translates the page on your phone. The page's text isn't sent anywhere. The first time you use a pair of languages, their files download once (tens of MB) and stay on your phone.

When Wayward offers

On a page in a language you don't read, a bar asks "Translate this page from Spanish?" (with the page's language). Tap Translate, or the X to close the bar.

The bar's ⋮ (TalkBack: More translation choices) has:

  • Choose languages, to pick other languages first,
  • "Always translate" the language, so pages in it are translated without asking,
  • "Never translate" the language,
  • Never translate this site.

Which languages you read, and the lists of languages and sites you chose to always or never translate, are in Settings > Languages.

Translate any page yourself

  1. Open the ⋮ menu and tap Translate. Or tap the address bar and tap the translate icon in the field (TalkBack: Translate page).
  2. Check From and Into. Tick "Always translate" if you want that language done without asking from now on. The first time for a pair of languages, the dialog shows how big the download is.
  3. Tap Translate. A bar says "Translating into English…" (or your language) while it works.

Go back to the original

On a translated page the menu's Translate is switched on. Tap it again to see the original. The translate icon in the address field reads Show original then, and does the same.

The menu's Translate works even with the offer bar turned off in Settings > Languages > Offer to translate pages.
