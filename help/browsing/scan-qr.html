Scan a QR code

How-to

Scan a QR code with your camera. Wayward can open the address in the code or search for its text.

  1. Tap the address bar.
  2. Tap Scan next to the field (TalkBack: Scan QR code).
  3. The first time, Android asks whether Wayward may use the camera. Allow it.
  4. Point the camera at the code. The code is read on your phone, and nothing is saved or sent.

What you'll see

  • A web address: Open this address? shows the address in full, so you can check where it goes before you tap Open. Copy copies it instead.
  • A short line of words: QR code with Search and Copy.
  • Anything else (Wi-Fi details, a contact card, a sign-in code, or an address that looks odd or very long): Wayward only offers Copy, and says it isn't a web address or words to search.

If the camera isn't allowed

The scanner says Allow the camera to scan. with an Allow button, which brings back Android's question. If you said no before and Android won't ask again, the scanner points you to your phone's settings instead: tap App settings, then allow the camera for Wayward under its permissions.
