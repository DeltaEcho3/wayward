Keys and costs

Which AI providers does the assistant work with?

OpenRouter (one account with access to many companies' models), Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, and xAI (Grok). You use your own account there, and the provider bills you for what you use. Pick one in Settings > AI assistant > AI provider. See set up the assistant.

What does the assistant cost?

There are two costs to consider. A one-time Wayward Premium purchase unlocks the assistant. Using it costs whatever your AI provider charges for the tokens each request uses, billed to your account there. Before a page is shared, the card shows an estimate of the request's size, and a task's card estimates the whole task. These are only estimates, so set a spending limit with your provider (Set a spending limit opens its page). See set up the assistant.

Is my API key safe in Wayward?

Your API key stays encrypted on your phone with a key from Android's keystore, and is only ever sent to that provider's own address. It isn't in a settings backup either. If you think it got out, delete it at your provider (that stops it working everywhere) and tap Disconnect in Wayward, which deletes it here. See set up the assistant.

Can I use the assistant without my own key, or with a model on my phone?

Not today. The assistant requires your own API key from one of the four providers, and it doesn't run models on the phone. Only turning a video's speech into text happens on your phone. See set up the assistant.

It says my key was rejected, or my account is out of credits. What now?

Your AI provider rejected the request. A rejected key may have been deleted or mistyped: paste it again in Settings > AI assistant (for OpenRouter, connect again). "Out of credits" means your account needs topping up at the provider. "Too many requests" means you reached the provider's limit for a while; try again in a moment, or check your account. See set up the assistant.

Why did changing the model start a new chat?

Each model uses its own chat format, so Changing the provider or model starts a new conversation. With a new model, your earlier messages stay on screen, but the model doesn't see them, and the chat says so when you send your next message. A new provider deletes the chat right away, so save it first if you want to keep it. See save, open or delete a chat.

Why can't it look at the page?

Some models can read text only. Settings > AI assistant > Model says whether yours can see screenshots. A text-only model still reads the page's text, links and buttons; pick another model if you want it to look too. See set up the assistant.

What's sent, and staying safe

What does the assistant send to the AI, and when?

Your AI provider receives what you share through the assistant, and the few things below that go without asking. Before it reads a page, the sharing card shows the site, who gets it (your provider and model), what goes (the page's text, links and buttons, any screenshots it takes, a video's captions) and the size. None of the page's text, links, buttons, screenshots or captions goes until you tap Share, unless you chose Always share pages, tapped Share, and don't ask again for this site for that site, or let it act on its own (Always allow, or a task you OK'd). A page on your local network, like a router or a printer, always gets the card. Your messages and the chat so far go with each request. A Share covers that page until your message is done, at its address without anything after a # in it, so a page that changes what it shows there is read again without asking; another page, or your next message, asks again. A few things go without the card, in every mode: the address and title of the page it's on, after it opens one, taps, types or goes back, its searches' results, and what its video and download tools find (the videos on the page, with their sites and sizes; a video's title and qualities, with its page's address; and, while Downloads is unlocked, the titles of your latest downloads, never a private tab's). What it did under a message shows what went. See ask about a page.

Does Wayward send my browsing to the AI when I'm not using it?

No. Browsing on its own sends nothing to your AI provider. Only what you share while using the assistant goes, and, while it works on a message, the few things above that go without the card: the address and title of a page it opens, its searches' results, and what its video and download tools find. Private tabs are never shared, and a locked tab only after you've unlocked it. See ask about a page.

What's hidden in screenshots it takes?

Form fields, and anything typed in them, are covered before the assistant sends a screenshot. Other sites' frames inside the page are covered too, unless you tapped Share on that page's card: then they show. A model that can't see pictures gets no screenshots at all. See let it do things, with your OK.

Can the assistant sign in, buy things or type my passwords?

The assistant never enters passwords, card numbers or one-time codes. In every mode it asks first before opening an address that didn't come from you or a page it read, and before pressing or submitting anything that reads as a log-in, a purchase, a deletion or sending things in your name (buttons like Send, Post or Confirm). It knows those by their words and by a form's password or card field, so a button worded some other way can go without asking under Always allow or a task you OK'd. It does act with your sign-ins in regular tabs, as you would, so keep it off pages with information you wouldn't want shared. See let it do things, with your OK.

Can a web page trick the assistant?

It can try. A page can hide instructions in its text, hoping the assistant follows them. Wayward limits what that can do: the assistant asks before typing or pressing on a different site from the one it read, before a web search once it has read a page, before acting inside another site's frame, and before opening addresses a page made up, and text from another app asks before its links open. In any mode it follows plain links without asking, a couple of them to another site after it has read one. With Always allow, or during a task you OK'd, it can also press buttons on the site it's reading without asking. On sites where you're signed in to something that matters, keep How it acts on Ask each time. See let it do things, with your OK.

Can it run commands on my phone or change my settings?

No. The assistant can't run commands on your phone or change Wayward's settings, Shields, locks, saved tasks or files. It works only on web pages in regular tabs, and on your downloads. It saves a video you ask for, and a link or button it taps that starts a download saves that file, as your own tap would. It can check on your latest downloads at any time, which sends their titles to your provider while Downloads is unlocked (never a private tab's). See let it do things, with your OK.

Why doesn't the assistant work in private tabs?

The assistant never reads private tabs, to keep their contents private. In a private tab its panel says The assistant doesn't work in private tabs, and that nothing from a private tab is sent to your AI provider; open the page in a regular tab to ask about it. See use private tabs.

Why did it stop at a "verify you're human" page?

In every mode, the assistant stops at bot checks and tells you, rather than trying to get past them. Answer the check yourself, then ask again. See let it do things, with your OK.

Does my AI provider keep what I send?

Your provider's terms and privacy policy govern what it keeps. Wayward has no copy on any server. See ask about a page.

Chats

Why does a long chat slow down, or say it's too long?

Each request includes your new message and the conversation so far, so a long chat means bigger requests, which take longer and cost more. Every model also has a limit on how much it can read at once. Past it, the assistant says so plainly, with Save chat and New chat under the message. Save it if you want to keep it, then start a new chat. See save, open or delete a chat.

Can I save or export a chat?

Yes. In the panel, tap ⋮, then Save chat. Choose Save as plain text, or Encrypt to lock the file with a password of 12 characters or more. To read an encrypted one later, use Open saved chat in the panel's ⋮ menu. See save, open or delete a chat.

Is my chat still there after I close Wayward?

Yes. Wayward keeps the chat on your phone, encrypted, and restores it when you reopen the assistant. It's deleted by New chat, Delete chat, Emergency delete, Exit Wayward, Delete everything when Wayward closes, clearing all your history, turning the assistant off, changing its provider (a settings file you restore can change it too), or Premium ending. See save, open or delete a chat.

Why is my chat locked?

If the assistant read a locked tab (it can once you've unlocked it), or you lock a tab it read, its chat locks whenever that tab locks (This chat used a locked tab), and one that holds something from Downloads locks whenever Downloads is locked (This chat holds something from Downloads). Tap Unlock to see it, or start a new chat. See locks.

What is References in the menu?

It lists the sites the assistant read in this chat. Wayward keeps the list with the chat, encrypted. The assistant uses it to ask before typing or pressing on a different site. Delete list clears it, but then the assistant asks before every field and button until you start a new chat. See save, open or delete a chat.
