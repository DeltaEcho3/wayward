Compare your tabs

How-to

Ask the assistant to compare two to four open tabs, such as product pages, hotel listings or recipes. It's good for "which is the better value?".

  1. Open the pages you want to compare, each in its own regular tab.
  2. Open the assistant's panel. In an empty chat, tap Compare tabs.
  3. Pick the tabs ("Pick 2 to 4 tabs."). Your question starts as Which is the better choice, and why?; change it to what you want to know.
  4. Tap Compare.
  5. A card asks Share pages?, naming the sites, your provider and the size of the request. Tap Share, or Don't share.

While it works, the chat says Reading the pages you picked, then the answer comes. Follow-up questions in the same chat know those pages.

If you closed one of the tabs meanwhile, nothing is compared, and the chat says so. If a page couldn't be read, open that tab once and try again.
