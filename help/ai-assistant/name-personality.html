Name it and set how it talks

How-to

Choose what to call your assistant and how you want it to talk.

  1. Open the ⋮ menu, then Settings > AI assistant.
  2. Under Name and personality, fill in Name (up to 24 characters; it starts as Assistant) and How it talks (like Friendly and brief).

The name shows on its panel and its cards, and in its step lines, and it answers as that name. How it talks only changes its tone: it still asks before the same things, and its cards still say plainly what will happen.

On-phone transcription

Further down, On-phone transcription says whether your phone can turn a video's speech into text by itself (Available on this phone or Not available on this phone). The assistant uses it for videos without captions, and for asking about a video you saved. The sound stays on your phone; only the text goes to your AI provider. It needs Android 13 or later and a speech service on the phone (most phones with Google's apps have one).
