When a download doesn't work

Why is there no download button on this page?

Start the video, then check again. Some pages only load it during playback. A video can also be one Wayward can't save, like a protected one (below). A live picture is never offered: your own camera's preview, a video call, or anything else a page shows live from a camera or microphone, since there's no file to save. And in this version of Wayward, videos from one site can't be saved at all. Files and pictures you can still save from a link: hold it and tap Download link. See save a video from a page.

Why does the first video download take a moment longer?

Wayward's video downloader starts only when it's needed, not every time Wayward opens, which saves your phone's memory. So the first video download, or the first check of a video's qualities, after Wayward starts takes a moment longer while it gets going. The ones after that are quicker. See save a video from a page.

Did clearing data or Exit Wayward stop my download?

No. Downloads under way pause for a moment while Wayward clears, then go on by themselves; some files you started from a page's own download link start again from the beginning. If Android closed Wayward first, they go on when you next open it. A page being saved as a PDF is the exception: it can't be paused, so a clear that closes its page stops it (see What happens to a page I'm saving as a PDF when I clear data or exit?). See Will clearing data or Exit Wayward stop a download that's under way?

What does "Play the video first, then download it." mean?

The page's player only asks for its video once it plays, so Wayward has nothing to download until playback starts. Close the download options, start the video, then tap the download button again (or the tab, or Download video in the ⋮ menu, whichever you use). Its qualities are there now. See save a video from a page.

What does "This video is protected and can't be downloaded." mean?

The video is copy protected (DRM) or uses an encrypted stream. Wayward never gets around copy protection, so it offers nothing to download. You can usually still watch it on the page, as long as Play protected video is on in Settings > Video and audio. See full screen, background play and picture-in-picture.

What does "Wayward can't save this player's video." mean?

A script on the page sends the video to the player in pieces, so there's no address Wayward could save it from. Nothing is offered for that video. See save a video from a page.

What do I do when qualities can't be read?

If the panel can't list the available video qualities, it says why, and offers Download best quality and Download as GIF. Try the first. If that fails too, its message in Downloads says why. See save a video from a page.

What does "The link expired or the site refused it." mean?

Video links on many sites expire. Open the page again and try once more, so Wayward gets a fresh link. Wayward already tries a few ways (including through the browser itself, as your tab would) before it says this. See find, pause and delete downloads.

What does "Parts of the video didn't download. Tap Retry." mean?

Some parts of the video are missing, or the file came out clearly shorter than the video. Wayward doesn't keep it as finished. Tap Retry in the download's ⋮ to get it again. See find, pause and delete downloads.

Why did a download fail in one of my spaces?

A space that keeps its sign-ins apart can't lend them to a download that uses a direct address, so a site that needs you signed in may refuse it. The row then says to try it from Main. Open the page in a Main tab and download it there. See spaces.

Why does a download say "Continues when you open Wayward"?

Android closed Wayward during a download that uses the browser, which needs Wayward open. It keeps what it has, and goes on by itself once you open Wayward. See find, pause and delete downloads.

Why did my download start over when I resumed it?

Some sites can't resume a partially downloaded file. Then Wayward says This site can't resume, so the download started again. and downloads it from the start. See find, pause and delete downloads.

A download keeps failing. What can I try?

Open the download's ⋮ menu and tap Retry. If it still fails, reload the page and start the download again from there, check your connection (a download waiting for one says Waiting for connection), and try another quality. If it never works on one site, tell us in Settings > Send feedback, picking Download problem. See find, pause and delete downloads.

Saving, sharing and the rules

Is it OK to download videos?

Only download what you have the right to, and follow the terms of the sites you use. That's in Wayward's Terms of Use too (section 7). Wayward never gets around copy protection: a protected video can't be downloaded. Whether saving a particular video is allowed depends on the site's terms and where you live, and this isn't legal advice. See save a video from a page.

Where do my downloads go?

By default, files stay inside Wayward, behind the downloads lock. Your gallery and other apps can't see them. You can send new downloads to a folder of your own instead in Settings > Downloads > Save downloads to. A private tab's downloads always stay inside. See choose where downloads go.

Why can't my gallery or other apps see a download?

Other apps can't see files stored inside Wayward: that's on purpose. To use one elsewhere, tap its ⋮ in Downloads: Export to gallery saves a copy your gallery can see (videos go to Movies/Wayward, pictures to Pictures/Wayward, sounds to Music/Wayward), and Move to folder moves it to a folder you pick. Share from the selection bar sends it straight to another app. Wayward asks first (unless you ticked Don't ask again), since the file leaves its lock. See choose where downloads go.

Why won't a saved video attach to an email?

Email apps have attachment size limits, often about 20 to 25 MB, and videos are usually bigger. Pictures and short clips attach fine. For a video, share it through a cloud storage app, or send a link to the page instead. See choose where downloads go.

How do I get back to the page a download came from?

Open the download's ⋮ menu and tap Source. The page opens in a new tab, in the same space (or a private tab, for a private tab's download). See find, pause and delete downloads.

Does Wayward remove a photo's location when I download or upload it?

Yes, by default. Pictures you download, copy or share, and the copy a site gets when you upload a photo, lose where they were taken (the GPS position, and the place's name: its city, state, country or location), the camera's make, model and serial numbers, and Facebook's photo ID. The picture itself doesn't change, and the photo on your phone is never touched. JPEG pictures are cleaned in full, and PNG and WebP pictures in their EXIF data. Wayward doesn't edit HEIC and AVIF photos, TIFF and camera raw files, or the XMP (an editing app's notes) in a PNG, WebP or GIF, so when one of those can say where it was taken, it counts as a picture Wayward couldn't clean: a HEIC or AVIF with EXIF or XMP details (a phone's HEIC photo has them), any TIFF or camera raw file (Canon's CR3, Fujifilm's RAF, Panasonic's RW2 and Olympus's ORF among them), and a PNG, WebP or GIF whose XMP holds a location or camera. A HEIC or AVIF with no such details, as many web pictures are, has nothing to take out. So does, once in a while, a picture put together in a way Wayward can't edit safely. Then it says so rather than pass it on: Share image and Copy image ask Share this picture as it is? or Copy this picture as it is? (Share anyway or Copy anyway, or Cancel), an upload asks Upload the photo as it is? (Upload anyway, or Don't upload, which sends the site nothing), and a downloaded picture's row in Downloads says Location and camera details not removed. A GIF has no other standard place for a location, so one without such XMP stays as it is. To keep it all, turn off Settings > Shields & Privacy > Remove photo location and camera. See the other privacy settings.

Why did Wayward ask before downloading an app file?

An app file, such as an .apk, can install software on your phone, and apps from outside Google Play can harm your phone or take your data. So Wayward asks Download this app? first, naming the site. Only download one if you trust the site. See download files, pictures and PDFs.

Why did Wayward ask to send notifications when I started a download?

Wayward uses notifications to show download progress, so it asks Android for permission the first time you download something, not when you first open the app. Say no and downloads still work; you just won't see them in your notifications. See download settings.

Can I pause a download, or limit how many run at once?

Yes. Tap the ring around a running download's icon to pause it, and tap it again to continue. Settings > Downloads > Downloads at once sets how many run together (3 to start with); the rest wait their turn. See find, pause and delete downloads.

Can old downloads delete themselves?

Yes. Go to Settings > Downloads > Delete saved files after (1, 7 or 30 days). It only deletes files kept inside Wayward, never files in your own folder. See download settings.
