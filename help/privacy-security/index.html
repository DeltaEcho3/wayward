Shields and ad blocking

How does Wayward block ads? Do I need another ad blocker?

No other blocker needed. Wayward has uBlock Origin built in, and it blocks ads and trackers on every page, in regular tabs, private tabs and link previews alike. On top of that the browser engine's own tracking protection, fingerprinting protection and link cleaning are on. The shield in the address bar counts what was blocked on each page. See see what was blocked.

A site is broken, blank, or won't let me sign in. What can I do?

Tap the shield in the address bar. Turn off Protection on this site. The page reloads with nothing blocked, for that site only. If that fixes it, leave it off there. If you turned on Block JavaScript on every site, turn it back on for that site with Block JavaScript on this site. See let a broken site through.

What do Standard, Strict and Maximum add?

Standard blocks ads, trackers, malware and cookie notices. Strict also blocks many more malware sites (the URLhaus list, which takes more of your phone's memory), phishing sites, tracking in links, sites reaching into your home network, "open in app" banners and social media widgets. Maximum also hides pop-ups, chat boxes, newsletter and notification prompts. Each step catches more and breaks a few more sites. See choose how much Wayward blocks.

Why did the URLhaus malware list move to Strict?

The URLhaus malware list, over 100,000 addresses, moved from Standard to Strict because it takes a lot of your phone's memory. Standard still blocks malware with uBlock Origin's own list. If your lists were exactly Standard, they changed to the new Standard by themselves; lists you changed yourself stay as they were. For URLhaus, pick Strict. See choose how much Wayward blocks.

Can I add a filter list by its address, or write my own rules?

Not on Wayward's Filter lists screen: it lets you switch the lists uBlock Origin already knows. uBlock Origin's own dashboard can do both, for people who know it: open the shield, tap Ad blocking controls, and open its dashboard from there. Its Filter lists page can import a list from an address, and My filters takes rules of your own. Wayward keeps what you add there when you change levels. A list you add yourself can break sites, so add only ones you trust. See choose how much Wayward blocks.

Can I hide one annoying part of a page?

Yes. Open the shield, tap Ad blocking controls, and use uBlock Origin's element picker to pick the part to hide. For pop-ups that cover pages on many sites, turn on a list instead: see hide pop-ups and overlays.

Can I install add-ons or extensions?

No. Wayward doesn't support add-ons, for security and to keep the app easier to maintain. What people usually install them for is built in: ad and tracker blocking (uBlock Origin), dark mode for websites, translation, reader view and video downloads. See hide pop-ups and overlays.

What does the blocked count mean? Can I hide it?

The count shows how many ads and trackers Wayward blocked in regular tabs this week, counted on your phone. Tap it for the count by day and the names blocked most. Nothing about it leaves your phone. To hide it from the start page, turn off Ad blocking in Settings > Customize > Show on the home page. See use the start page.

Why do I still see some cookie banners?

Wayward hides cookie notices at Standard and higher blocking levels, but some sites build theirs in a way the lists don't catch yet. You can close them as usual. See choose how much Wayward blocks.

A "verify you're human" check keeps coming back. What can I do?

Turn off Protection on this site for that site, then reload. Blocking can interfere with some checks. Wayward doesn't answer these checks for you. See let a broken site through.

Why won't a site load over plain http?

HTTPS-only is on, so Wayward requires a secure HTTPS connection. A site without https gets a page saying so, with Open anyway (not secure) if you still want it, for that site in that tab. See secure connections and the other settings.

What is Secure DNS, and which provider should I pick?

Your phone looks up a site's name before it can reach the site. Secure DNS encrypts those lookups so your network can't read or log them. Wayward starts on Quad9. You can pick Cloudflare or Mullvad instead, or Custom for a provider of your own. It only covers Wayward, and it isn't a VPN: sites still see your IP address. See secure connections and the other settings.

What does fingerprinting protection do?

Sites can recognize your phone from small details it shares, like its screen and fonts. Block fingerprinting (on) mixes those details up. Strict fingerprinting protection (off to start with) goes further, the way Tor Browser does: Wayward tells sites the same as every other browser with it on. Some sites break, times show in UTC, and sites see English and a light theme, so turn it on only if you want that trade. See secure connections and the other settings.

Private tabs, spaces and locks

What does a private tab keep, and what does it wipe?

Wayward saves neither the tab nor its browsing history. Its cookies and site data are wiped when your last private tab closes, when Wayward closes, or when you clear browsing data, so you're signed out of whatever you signed in to there. Bookmarks and downloads you make in a private tab stay. So do shortcuts you add there, on the private start page only: it has its own shortcuts, which never show on your regular start page. See use private tabs.

Does a private tab hide me from my internet provider, my employer or the sites I visit?

No. Private tabs only keep your visit off your phone. The sites you visit still see your IP address, and whoever runs your network (your internet provider, or an employer or school) can still see which sites you go to. Secure DNS hides your lookups from the network, but it isn't a VPN. See use private tabs.

Why does Wayward ask for my fingerprint before a private tab opens?

Lock private tabs is on (the default if your phone has a screen lock). While it is, every way into private tabs asks first, even the first private tab. One unlock opens them all until they lock again. To stop the question, turn the lock off in Settings > Tabs and History; that asks for the unlock too. See locks.

Why does Wayward look blank or blurred in recent apps, or block screenshots?

Hide in recent apps is on, so the app switcher shows Wayward blank. Without it, Wayward shows blurred there while a private tab is open. Screenshots are blocked in private tabs (Screenshots is Not in private tabs), and always on a locked tab, an open locked space or locked Downloads. Both settings are in Settings > Shields & Privacy, under Privacy. See secure connections and the other settings.

What are spaces for?

Use spaces to keep tabs for work, home or a trip apart. With Keep sign-ins separate on, a space has its own cookies, site data, sign-ins and permissions, so you can be signed in to one site as two people, one per space. Each can have its own color and its own lock. See keep sign-ins apart with spaces.

What don't spaces keep apart?

History, bookmarks, downloads, Shields and uBlock Origin's settings are shared across all spaces. Clear browsing data and Shred reach every space. A space that shares Main's sign-ins shares Main's cookies too. See keep sign-ins apart with spaces.

A locked tab opened without asking, or locked again at once. Why?

Locked tabs lock again in Settings > Tabs and History controls when you need to unlock again. With Right away (the start), a locked tab, space or your private tabs lock as soon as you go back to the Tabs screen or another tab, and Downloads as soon as you leave it. On After 30 seconds they stay open for half a minute after you look away, so coming straight back doesn't ask again. Leaving Wayward locks them either way (private tabs after a minute). See locks.

Clearing data

How do I clear my history, cookies and cache?

Open the ⋮ menu and tap Clear data at the bottom. Select the data you want to clear (History, Cookies and site data, Cached images and files and more), then tap Clear. To clear only a stretch of history, like the last hour, use the broom on the History screen. See clear data, Shred and Emergency delete.

Will clearing data delete my bookmarks or downloads?

No. Bookmarks, start page shortcuts you added, saved tasks, downloads and settings are kept. That includes the private start page's own shortcuts, which Shred, Emergency delete, Exit Wayward and Delete everything when Wayward closes delete along with your regular ones. Not even Emergency delete touches bookmarks, downloads, saved tasks or settings. To delete a download, do it in Downloads. See clear data, Shred and Emergency delete.

Will clearing data or Exit Wayward stop a download that's under way?

No. Clear data (with Cookies and site data ticked), Emergency delete, Exit Wayward and Delete everything when Wayward closes pause your downloads for a moment while they clear, then the downloads go on by themselves. Some files you started from a page's own download link start again from the beginning. If Android ends Wayward before they go on, they go on when you next open it. Clearing without Cookies and site data ticked, like only your history or the cache, doesn't pause them at all. See clear data, Shred and Emergency delete.

What happens to a page I'm saving as a PDF when I clear data or exit?

It depends on whether the clear closes the page. Wayward makes the PDF from the open page, so it can't be paused and picked up later the way a download can. Clear data that leaves the page's tab open doesn't interrupt it, even with Cookies and site data ticked: the PDF is finished and Wayward says Saved to Downloads. Emergency delete, Exit Wayward, and Clear data with the page's tab ticked (Tabs, or Private tabs for a private tab) close the page, so the PDF isn't saved and Wayward says This page couldn't be saved as a PDF. Shred closes the site's tabs, and Clear this space and Delete space close the space's tabs, so a PDF being saved from one of those tabs stops the same way. With Delete everything when Wayward closes on, swiping Wayward away stops it too, usually without a message. Nothing half-made shows in Downloads. Open the page again and tap Save as PDF once more. Once Wayward has said Saved to Downloads, the PDF is a download like any other, and no clear deletes it. See desktop site, zoom, print and save as PDF.

Does clearing data delete text Wayward kept for me?

Yes. The text you typed in a page and hadn't sent, which Wayward keeps so it can put it back if the page loads again, is deleted whenever you clear browsing data, whatever you tick. Emergency delete, Exit Wayward and Delete everything when Wayward closes delete it too. See text you hadn't sent yet.

Can I clear just one site?

Yes. Tap the shield in the address bar, then "Shred" and the site's name. It closes that site's tabs and deletes its cookies, logins, site data, cache, permissions, history and start page shortcuts, and keeps its bookmarks. Shred automatically does it by itself once none of the site's pages are open. See clear data, Shred and Emergency delete.

What does Emergency delete do, and what does it keep?

It closes all tabs, including private tabs, and deletes all your browsing data, saved tabs, the assistant's chat, the start page shortcuts you added, and your spaces apart from Main. It keeps bookmarks, saved tasks, downloads and settings. It asks once, then can't be undone. It's in the Tabs screen's ⋮ menu. See clear data, Shred and Emergency delete.

Why am I signed out of sites every time?

One of these is usually why:

  • Delete everything when Wayward closes is on (Settings > Tabs and History), so each time Wayward closes your sign-ins go.
  • You signed in from a private tab. Those sign-ins go when your last private tab or Wayward closes. With Private by default on, links from other apps open privately too.
  • You signed in from a space with its own sign-ins. That space keeps them; your other spaces don't see them.
  • Shred automatically is on for that site.

See use private tabs.

Can Wayward delete everything whenever I close it?

Yes. Enable Settings > Tabs and History > Delete everything when Wayward closes. Swiping Wayward away in recent apps, or Android closing it, then deletes all tabs, cookies, logins, site data, cache, permissions, history, saved tabs and the start page shortcuts you added. Bookmarks, saved tasks, downloads and settings stay. See clear data, Shred and Emergency delete.

What's encrypted, and what Wayward connects to

What does Wayward encrypt on my phone?

  • Wayward stores history and bookmarks in separate encrypted databases (SQLCipher). Each database has its own key protected by Android's keystore. Clearing your history destroys its key, so nothing of it can be read back.
  • Your open tabs, saved so they reopen next time, with text you typed in a page and hadn't sent yet, encrypted with a key that Android's keystore protects.
  • Your saved tasks, the assistant's chat, your list of spaces and the start page's site icons, each in an encrypted file of Wayward's own (AES-GCM), with a key from Android's keystore.
  • Your AI provider keys, encrypted with a key from Android's keystore.

Keys in Android's keystore never leave your phone. See lock Wayward down.

Is the text Wayward keeps from a page encrypted?

Yes. When you type in a page's text box and haven't sent it yet, Wayward keeps a copy with the tab so it can put it back if the page has to load again. That copy is encrypted on your phone with your saved tabs, is never sent anywhere, and the assistant can't read it. It's never kept from private tabs, password, card or code boxes, or text with a card number in it. See text you hadn't sent yet.

What isn't encrypted by Wayward itself?

Wayward does not encrypt cookies, site data, the browser cache or downloaded files. Android's own storage encryption covers them, as it does all of Wayward's storage. Files you save to a folder on your phone are outside Wayward, where other apps can see them. See choose where downloads go.

Do the locks encrypt anything?

No. Locking a tab, your private tabs, a space or Downloads controls who can see them: they ask for your phone's fingerprint, face or PIN first. What's behind a lock is stored the same way as without it. See locks.

How is a saved chat file protected?

When you save a chat with Encrypt, you choose the password (at least 12 characters) that protects the file, using AES-256-GCM with a key made from your password with PBKDF2 (600,000 rounds). Wayward doesn't keep the password, so if you lose it, nobody can open the file, us included. Anyone holding the file can keep guessing, so use a long password. Save as plain text isn't encrypted at all. See save, open or delete a chat.

Does Wayward collect anything about me?

No. Wayward doesn't report your browsing to its makers or send them the sites you visit, your downloads, your cookies or what's on pages, and there are no analytics, ads or tracking in the app. We have no servers that receive any of it. Feedback is optional, and you see the whole report before you send it. The AI assistant is the exception you choose: when you use it, what you share goes to the AI provider you picked: see ask about a page.

Who does Wayward connect to, and why?

In addition to the sites you visit, Wayward connects mainly to these services.

  • Your search engine receives searches and, unless you turn suggestions off, requests as you type.
  • Your Secure DNS provider looks up sites. The default is Quad9.
  • Wayward checks uBlock Origin's list servers about once an hour for filter updates.
  • Mozilla provides data the browser engine keeps up to date (such as revoked certificate lists), a check that your connection works, and the translation files the first time you translate.
  • Google Safe Browsing helps check addresses against lists of dangerous sites. On a match, it receives a short part of a hash of the address. It never receives the address itself.
  • Wayward connects to Google Play when you buy or restore Premium.
  • Your AI provider receives requests only when you use the assistant.
  • Your image search service receives requests only when you search for a picture.

The privacy policy lists every one, with what's sent: in Wayward, it's Settings > About Wayward > Legal information > Wayward Privacy Policy, and it's on this site too. See secure connections and the other settings.

Can I sync my bookmarks, tabs or passwords with another device?

Not today. Wayward has no account or sync feature, so Wayward keeps nothing of yours on a server. To move bookmarks, use Export bookmarks and Import bookmarks (see bookmarks). Your passwords stay with your phone's password manager, which may sync them itself (see sign in with your password manager).
