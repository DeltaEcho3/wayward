Hide pop-ups and overlays on pages

How-to

Some sites cover what you're reading with sign-up forms, notification requests or chat boxes. Wayward has no add-ons, but its built-in uBlock Origin has lists made for exactly this.

Turn on the pop-up list

  1. Open the ⋮ menu, then Settings > Shields & Privacy.
  2. Tap Filter lists.
  3. Scroll to Annoyances and turn on "AdGuard – Popup Overlays".

The same section has more lists for other annoyances, like "EasyList – Newsletter Notices", "EasyList – Chat Widgets" and "EasyList – Notifications". Turning on several at once is what the Maximum level of Ad blocking does, so you can pick Maximum instead (see choose how much Wayward blocks).

If a site breaks

Annoyance lists hide more of each page, so now and then they hide something you need. If a site stops working, turn off Protection on this site from its shield button, or turn the list off again. See let a broken site through.

Hide one thing yourself

To hide a single part of one page, tap the shield, then Ad blocking controls, and use its element picker to pick what to hide.

Cookie notices are hidden from the Standard level up, so you shouldn't need a list for those, though a few sites still get theirs through.
