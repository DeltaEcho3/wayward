The short version

Wayward does not send us your browsing history, the websites you visit, the videos or files you download, your cookies, or the contents of web pages. We don't run analytics, ads or tracking in the app. Support reports are optional, and you see everything in one before you send it. The AI assistant is different: when you use it, the content you choose to share is sent to OpenRouter and the AI provider you picked.

What stays on your phone

  • Browsing. Wayward always browses privately. History, cookies and site data are kept only while the app runs and are wiped when you close it or clear browsing data.
  • Downloads. Saved inside the app by default, behind your fingerprint, face or PIN if you keep the downloads lock on. If you choose a download folder, files are saved there instead, where other apps on your phone can see them.
  • Settings, your OpenRouter key and your Premium status. Stored on your phone; the key is encrypted with Android's keystore.

We have no servers that receive any of this.

The AI assistant

The assistant is off until you turn it on, and it uses your own OpenRouter account. Before it reads any page, Wayward asks you, shows the site and the AI provider, and lets you see exactly what would be sent. If you say no, nothing from that page is sent. OpenRouter and the AI provider process your requests under their own privacy policies and retention practices, which may differ from ours. Don't use the assistant on pages with personal or sensitive information.

Support reports

Reports are off until you create one, and nothing is sent automatically. A report contains only: the report type and area you choose, the app version and build, the browser engine version, your Android major version, a short error code with a rough time (like "today"), and any description you type. Links and file paths are removed from your description, but anything else you type is sent as written. You send the report yourself through your phone's share sheet, usually by email.
